Common questions.

Is GameRemote available in the App Store?

GameRemote for iPhone, iPad and Mac is still in development. There is no public App Store download or confirmed release date yet.

What do I need to connect?

A compatible PS5 or PS4 that you own or are authorised to use, Remote Play enabled, your own games, and a reachable console on the same home network. The iPhone and iPad preview needs the console address, the console account's Account ID and a current pairing code.

Can I connect away from home?

The iPhone and iPad preview supports manual setup on your home network. It has no PSN sign-in, automatic console discovery or supported internet connection flow. Mac setup and connection options differ.

Which controller features are included?

The iPhone and iPad preview includes on-screen controls and basic paired game-controller input. Touchpad swipes, motion input, rumble, adaptive triggers and microphone input are not included. Physical controller compatibility and playback have not yet been verified.

Can I change video quality on iPhone or iPad?

The preview uses a fixed 720p, 60 fps target with H.264. It has no video-quality picker. The target does not guarantee delivered frame rate, latency or picture quality. Mac settings differ.

GameRemote Plus.

Subscriptions are being prepared for the App Store launch. They are not on sale yet.

The planned offer is a free download for setup, then an annual subscription for streaming on iPhone, iPad and the native Mac app. The UK launch price is £9.99 per year. Eligible subscribers will receive a 3-day free trial; Apple determines eligibility. The app and Apple's confirmation will show the applicable local price before you subscribe.

The trial converts to the annual subscription automatically unless cancelled. The annual subscription then renews automatically. Apple advises cancelling at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you do not want to be charged. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Use Manage Subscription in the app or Apple's subscription settings to manage renewal or cancel. To use an existing purchase on another supported Apple device, sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases in GameRemote. This Apple subscription does not currently cover Android or Windows.

GameRemote does not include a console or games. You need your own compatible console, games and suitable network. Subscription access does not guarantee network performance or support for every controller feature.

Make your report useful.

Include your device, operating system, app version, console model, the visible error message and the steps that led to it. A screenshot of the error can help if it contains no private details.

Do not send passwords, pairing codes, Account IDs, registration keys or PSN tokens. Review logs and exported settings before sharing them. Mac exports and logs can include console, network or account details. Android settings exports include pairing keys.

Email support is handled through iCloud Mail. See the email support privacy information before sending attachments.
