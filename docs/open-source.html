Open source, with credit.

GameRemote builds on the work of the remote-play community. Those contributions remain part of its identity.

Project origins.

GameRemote is a modified fork of chiaki-ng, based on the original Chiaki project. Upstream author, copyright, licence and third-party notices are retained.

The project source uses AGPL-3.0-only terms with a documented OpenSSL additional permission. The full texts describe their scope.

Third-party components.

Different platforms use different components. The iPhone and iPad implementation includes Opus, libevent, json-c, MiniUPnPc, Mbed TLS, curl, nanopb, GF-Complete and Jerasure. Their own licence and copyright notices apply.

The licence files linked here cover the project itself. Release-specific component notices belong with the matching app and source package.
