Privacy, explained by platform.

Last updated 7 October 2026. This policy covers the GameRemote website, email support and the current pre-release apps.

Who runs GameRemote.

GameRemote is developed and published independently by AHMED TAHA A A ALSAUXHY. For support or privacy requests, email saaaalahy@icloud.com.

The apps are still in development. The platform descriptions below cover their current implementations and may change before a public release.

Your console connection.

GameRemote receives video and audio from your selected PS5 or PS4, processes playback on your device and sends control input back to that console. Console addresses and connection information are used to establish the session.

Account setup, saved settings and diagnostic information differ by platform. The iPhone and iPad description should not be read as a description of Mac or Android behaviour.

iPhone and iPad.

The current iPhone and iPad app has no GameRemote account service, publisher analytics, advertising or tracking. Its console connection sends data to the console address you select; it does not send that console data to the developer.

  • The console nickname, address, console type and local record identifier are saved in the app's storage.
  • Your entered Account ID and pairing code are used to register with the selected console. They are not saved in the console record.
  • Pairing keys are saved in the device Keychain with device-only accessibility, available while the device is unlocked.
  • A console login PIN, if required, is passed to the active session and is not saved by the app.
  • Video, audio and control input are processed for the session. The app does not include microphone capture, media recording or a media-upload service.

Removing a saved console or resetting its saved data removes the associated app record and pairing keys. The app attempts to exclude console metadata from backup; this is not a guarantee about every device backup or migration. Physical-device backup, migration and deletion behaviour has not yet been verified.

Opening support, privacy or source links uses your browser and the services described below.

Mac.

The Mac app saves settings and registered-console information on your Mac. Optional Sony/PSN account setup opens Sony sign-in in your browser. The app exchanges authorisation codes and refresh tokens with Sony endpoints, and saves access tokens, refresh tokens and expiry information in application settings.

If you choose Public Lookup, the Mac app sends the PSN username you enter directly to psn.flipscreen.games to look up its Account ID. This is an optional third-party service, separate from Sony sign-in.

Optional internet play also contacts Sony's console-discovery and remote-session services. Connection setup fetches STUN server lists hosted on GitHub and can contact servers in those lists, with Moonlight and Google servers as fallbacks, to determine network addresses. These requests expose your network address to the contacted service. The source code does not establish how these services retain or reuse request information.

Sony and your browser handle sign-in information under their own privacy practices. The Mac implementation does not use the iPhone/iPad device-only Keychain storage described above.

The Mac app includes settings import/export and local diagnostic/session logs. These can contain console, network or account details. Review and remove private information before sharing them. A copy you export or send is also stored by the destination you choose.

Android source.

The Android version is also in development. Its source saves pairing records in a local database, including console nickname, MAC address, console type, available access-point details and pairing keys. It also saves manually added addresses and app settings. Registration uses an Account ID and pairing code; the Account ID help action opens an external upstream guide.

Local session logs can include connection and device details. The app keeps up to five older recognised logs when it creates a new one, so up to six may remain. This is based on log count, not a fixed number of days.

Android settings exports contain pairing keys and access-point details. Base64 encoding does not encrypt them. The app shows a pairing-key warning and asks for confirmation before sharing. The app or service you choose controls any subsequent upload or storage.

The current Android configuration disables automatic backup and excludes supported app-data categories from cloud backup and device transfer. This does not prevent manual exports or log sharing. Removing a console deletes its local registration record; clearing Android app data removes the app's private records, settings and logs. Copies already exported or shared must be removed separately. Release-device behaviour has not yet been verified.

AndroidX EmojiCompat requests an emoji font from a system-installed provider after startup or resume. That provider may download fonts. The provider and network destinations depend on the device.

Apple subscriptions.

The subscription-enabled iPhone, iPad and Mac builds being prepared for launch use Apple's StoreKit service. Apple handles purchase confirmation, billing, introductory-offer eligibility, subscription management and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy.

GameRemote reads Apple-verified subscription information on your device to decide whether streaming is unlocked. This includes the product, transaction status and relevant expiry or revocation information. The implementation does not send receipts or transaction identifiers to a GameRemote server. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Restoring purchases asks Apple to synchronise purchases for the Apple Account used in the App Store.

These billing builds are still in development; subscriptions are not on sale yet. See subscription support for the planned offer and cancellation information.

This website.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site files contain no analytics, advertising, JavaScript, contact form or external font. Images and styles are served with the site.

GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed into GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's processing and retention practices.

Following a link to GitHub, an upstream project or another external service takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Email support.

If you email saaaalahy@icloud.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments through Apple's iCloud Mail service. This information is used to read and respond to your request and investigate the issue you report.

Messages and attachments are stored in the support mailbox. Apple and your email provider process email delivery and storage under their own terms and privacy practices; see Apple's Privacy Policy. You can request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence using the contact below.

Include only what is needed to explain the issue. Do not send passwords, Account IDs, pairing codes, console keys or PSN tokens. Redact these details from screenshots, logs and exported settings before sharing.

Privacy requests.

For a question about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence, email saaaalahy@icloud.com. Identify the correspondence you mean without sending console credentials.

Console information stored only on your device is managed through the app and your device's settings. Requests about information held by GitHub, Sony, Apple or another provider are also subject to that provider's own procedures.
