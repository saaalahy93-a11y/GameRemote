A connection to your console.

GameRemote is designed to receive your console's picture and sound, then send your controls back. Bring your console, your games and a suitable network.

Pair on your home network
The iPhone and iPad preview uses a console address, Account ID and pairing code. It has no automatic discovery or PSN sign-in.
Choose your input
The iPhone and iPad preview includes on-screen controls and basic paired game-controller input. Advanced controller features are not included.
Know the current scope
The iPhone and iPad preview targets 720p at 60 fps using H.264. Actual performance depends on your device, console and network.
